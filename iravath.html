About

The "Iravath" rover is a cutting-edge project for the ISRO Robotic Competition 2024, developed within a five-month timeline. Its mechanical structure, 85% 3D-printed using PLA and ABS via FDM technology, features a robust Rocker-Bogie suspension system for exceptional mobility over rugged terrain. A six-steering mechanism ensures superior maneuverability in tight spaces, while a differential gear system enhances stability. Autonomous navigation, powered by machine learning and sensor fusion, enables the rover to adapt to dynamic environments in real-time. ROS on a Raspberry Pi, with an Arduino interface, coordinates the rover’s systems for precise control. The Iravath rover showcases advanced robotics, combining durable design and autonomous capabilities for space exploration and terrestrial applications.
