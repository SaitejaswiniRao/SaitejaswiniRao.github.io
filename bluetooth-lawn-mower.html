About

The Semi-Automatic Lawn Mower combines manual control with smart features for an efficient and user-friendly mowing experience. While the user guides the mower, it offers automated navigation with predefined mowing patterns and adjustable cutting heights. Equipped with a simple control panel and built-in safety sensors, the mower stops the blades if lifted or tilted. A powerful battery ensures long mowing sessions, and some models include basic obstacle detection to avoid damage from small obstacles. This design reduces effort while maintaining control, delivering a professional-looking cut. Balancing convenience and user involvement, the semi-automatic mower is perfect for those seeking efficiency without fully automated operation.
